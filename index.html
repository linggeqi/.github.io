02 / Core Advantages

多岗位融合的专业技能与灵活的处置方式 Versatile Skills & Adaptive Handling

拥有影视链条上多岗位的专业技能与工作经验，熟悉规范的同时也清楚本质。 以人为核心、以问题为导向，擅长工作沟通与技术流程指导。 Multi-role skills and hands-on experience across the film and video chain: I know the standards and the logic behind them. People-first and problem-oriented, I communicate well and guide technical workflows.

01

全面的岗位技能Well-Rounded Role Skills

能在相应的工作岗位上做好执行，更能在团队中进行准确高效的沟通。 I can execute well in my own role and communicate clearly and efficiently within the team.

多以摄影、灯光、录音、后期的岗位，服务于数人的精炼团队至三四十人的满配团队（短片、纪录片、TVC、导播）。 上可在做好自身所处岗位工作之余，使组间及工种间的沟通全面准确而不多余，提效组内指挥与工种间协作；下可为各个环节提供技术支持，或在某一环节出现问题或滞后时，做出合理的指挥规划，为项目推进予以全面的保障。整个链条的能力，既是我自身执行经验、自身与团队问题的总结，也是合作对象反哺的积累。
I have mostly worked in camera, lighting, sound-recording and post roles, serving lean teams of a few people up to full crews of thirty or forty (short films, documentaries, TVC and live broadcast direction). On the coordination side, beyond doing my own job well, I keep cross-department communication accurate and without excess, making on-set command and cross-role collaboration more efficient. On the execution side, I give technical support to every link and, when a link stalls or falls behind, plan and direct accordingly to safeguard the project. This whole-chain ability is the accumulation of my own execution experience, the lessons drawn from my own and the team's problems, and what collaborators have taught me in return.
02

良好的人员管理People Management

精准看人，恰当用人，良于待人。 Read people accurately, place them well, and treat them fairly.

工作中擅长快速而相对准确地判断人员工作习惯与能力水平，且有保持观察、持续分析的习惯，不让先入为主的印象影响工作与人员体验。 粗略论之，对于能力与主观能动性强者，予其恰当的权与空间；对于习惯听令执行者，予其准确的指令与交接标准。以高效协作为核心，针对不同对象调整沟通与协作习惯。擅长察觉团队情绪并进行管理与引导，保障良好的工作氛围与状态，优化合作体验，以保障优秀成员的留存率与合作方的后续合作意愿。
I read people's working habits and ability quickly and fairly accurately, and I keep observing and analyzing rather than letting first impressions affect work or the team's experience. Roughly: for capable, proactive members I grant the right authority and room; for those who follow instructions I give precise orders and handover standards. Centered on efficient collaboration, I adjust how I communicate and cooperate with different people. I sense, manage and guide team mood, keep a good working atmosphere, and improve the experience of cooperation, which helps retain good members and keeps partners willing to work with us again.
03

情绪与叙事组织规划能力Emotion & Narrative Planning

擅长以传达目的为核心，规划视听与叙事，组织情绪以服务于表达。 I plan the audiovisual and narrative around the intended message, and shape emotion to serve the expression.

从服务传达的角度而言，我能敏锐而准确地剖析出客户的核心需求，拂去认为需要但并不准确或高效的部分，并使其贴近可执行的现实条件，最终实现客户对流程与结果的满意与我方工作的顺畅。 从现场情绪的引导与后期组织而言，我擅长根据表达的方式与目的，处理拍摄团队的存在感与引导拍摄对象的情绪，以记录下恰当的画面与情绪；推进到后期制作之时，也擅长对视听效果的组织制作，像控制光比一样控制情绪对比的强度，以实现最终想传达的情感体验。
From the perspective of serving the message, I can dissect the client's core need, strip away what is thought necessary but not accurate or efficient, and bring it closer to workable reality, so the client is satisfied with both the process and the result while our work runs smoothly. On set I manage the crew's presence and guide the subjects' emotions according to the intended way and purpose, capturing the right images and feelings. In post I organize and craft audiovisual effects, controlling emotional contrast the way one controls light ratio, to deliver the emotional experience the work is meant to convey.
04

风险判断与应对Risk Judgment / Response

识别得了风险点，为它做好预案和准备。直面得了意外，为其做出恰当的处置。 I can identify risk points and prepare for them, and face surprises with the right response.

对风险的识别，最直接来源于丰富的过往经验，以及对法规与物理现实的持续了解，所以擅长预先察觉和分析出或明或暗的风险，当避则避，遇之则作出快速而恰当的解决方案。 面对意外，能情绪稳定地分析问题，以人员安全为最先优先级，分析不同方案可能造成的损害程度，及时止损，稳定人员情绪，做出恰当的指挥安排。
Risk awareness comes most directly from rich past experience and an ongoing understanding of regulations and physical reality, so I can sense and analyze both obvious and hidden risks, avoid what should be avoided, and respond quickly when they appear. Facing the unexpected, I stay calm, put people's safety first, weigh how much damage each option might cause, stop the loss in time, steady the crew, and make the right arrangement.
05

非标需求与技术探索Non-Standard Needs & Technical Exploration

熟悉标准化流程的同时，也擅长解决非标问题。勤于技术探索，也保持维稳的习惯。 I know standard workflows well and can solve non-standard problems; I explore technology actively while keeping delivery stable.

熟悉影视行业标准化的流程，对常规的需求能够高效满足，亦不拘泥于标准方案，擅长剖析需求的根本，为其定制出可落地可执行的方案。 喜欢技术探索而不囿于原地，习惯性地为新技术的加入或流程优化留出空间与时间，设置合理的容错区间，保障团队稳定交付的同时，也能小步快跑、持续进步。
I know the film industry's standard workflows and meet ordinary needs efficiently, yet I don't stick to standard solutions: I get to the root of a need and customize a workable, executable plan. I enjoy technical exploration instead of standing still, habitually reserving room and time for new technology or process improvements with a sensible margin for error, so the team delivers steadily while improving step by step.

04 / Work Method

稳定团队，运筹资源， 处置风险，交付结果。 Steady the team, marshal the resources, handle the risks, deliver the results.

个人的力量何其有限，流程方法便是由过去自身与合作者的工作经验与失误总结优化而来的武器，籍由其扩大影响，充分释放团队成员能力，增强整体的组织合作能力，是过去的总结，亦是未来优化的起点。 One person's strength goes only so far. Workflow is the weapon I keep refining from the experience and mistakes of my collaborators and myself — with it I multiply my influence, release the full ability of the team, and strengthen how the whole group works together. It is a summary of the past, and the starting point for every future improvement.

01

人员与流程调度Team / Workflow Coordination

规划并动态调控边界、节奏与节点，磨合陌生团队、润滑成熟团队，细至个人，调控整体。 Plan and keep re-balancing boundaries, rhythm and milestones — break in a fresh team, keep a seasoned one running smoothly, from each individual up to the whole crew.

  1. 确认关键岗位技能、熟练度与事件处置水平，了解人员的沟通习惯与现场状态，据此调整指令深度并安排具体工作
  2. 划清讲明权责与责任边界，排清道明任务优先级，讲明反馈口径
  3. 统一进度报告机制，掌握各组进度并动态调整任务分配
  4. 考量团队磨合程度，根据开组、中段、临杀青或是特殊拍摄对人员状态的影响，结合场次影响程度与拍摄强度，提前规划并动态调整场次与任务安排。
  1. Confirm each key role's skills, experience and how they handle problems; learn their communication habits and on-the-day condition, then set the level of instruction detail and assign the work.
  2. Draw and state the boundaries of authority and responsibility, lay out task priorities, and make the reporting and feedback channel explicit.
  3. Use one shared progress-reporting system, so I can see how every department is tracking and adjust assignments as things move.
  4. Account for how well the team has gelled — first days, mid-run, the run-up to wrap and special sequences all tax people differently — then weigh every scene against shooting intensity and plan, and re-plan, the order and workloads in advance.
02

技术与尝试Technical Trials

主动地对技术与流程持续不断的了解与优化，生生不息则前行不止 Keep learning and refining technology and workflow on my own initiative — a practice that keeps renewing itself is one that never stops moving forward.

  1. 保持对行业技术信息与社会环境的持续了解，分析新技术的有效性，辨别营销与炒作，了解政策与受众环境变化。
  2. 学习与采纳新技术时，应先展开小规模的试点，纳入现有工作体系时规划容错空间，保证整体调度稳定与损失可控。
  3. 流程上多与同行沟通学习，吸纳长处优化问题，并针对卡点与环境变化做适应性的优化调整，切忌懒政。
  4. 保持对观察发现的习惯，发现并归纳总结需求，对缺乏方案或是方案适用性差的需求，分配适量资源保持主动研究
  1. Stay current on industry technology and the wider environment; judge whether a new tool is genuinely useful, separate substance from hype, and track policy and audience shifts.
  2. When adopting something new, run a small pilot first; when folding it into the regular workflow, build in a margin for error so the overall schedule stays stable and any loss stays contained.
  3. Keep learning from peers — borrow their strengths, fix weak points — and adapt the workflow around bottlenecks and changing conditions, never letting laziness or inertia stall improvement.
  4. Keep observing and taking notes; when a real need appears that no solution, or no good solution, covers yet, put a sensible amount of resources into active research.
03

风险应对Risk Response

对风险保持谨慎，做到风险有预案，意外有保障。 Stay alert to risk — every risk has a plan, and every surprise has a fallback.

  1. 规划勘景阶段对可能存在的风险点与风险程度进行研判，小风险有预案，大风险当避则避。
  2. 对涉及人员安全的行动，采取“零信任”的方式监督保险措施与具体行动的执行。
  3. 超出预案的意外以人员安全为先，稳定人员状态并及时止损；与意外伴随机遇时，应确认风险可控的情况再予以挑战的行动。
  1. In planning and scouting, size up every risk and how serious it is — small risks get a contingency plan, big ones get avoided outright.
  2. For anything that touches people's safety, run supervision on a zero-trust basis, the way you would a handover: never take it on faith that the safeguards and the actual execution are in place until you have verified them yourself.
  3. When something exceeds the plan, put people's safety first, steady the crew and stop the loss in time; if an unexpected turn also brings an opportunity, only go for it once the risk is confirmed controllable.
04

团队情绪引导Team Emotion Guidance

常不被关注因素，但对长远发展至关重要——我重视，我保障。 Easy to overlook, yet it decides how far the team can grow — I take it seriously and protect it.

  1. 规划方案伊始便要考虑任务内容与强度对人员情绪与状态的影响，平衡进度排布与工作强度的关系，为团队成员创作劳逸结合的条件。
  2. 不将负面情绪带入工作，愈是管理之位愈需情绪稳定为先；及时遏制团队中产生的焦虑或浮躁情绪。
  3. 工作应阶段性复盘，但绝需杜绝马后炮。
  4. 指令目标清晰、目的明确，杜绝将人当作AI使用、杜绝发布目的不明、令人困惑的任务交由团队成员执行。
  5. 日常重视成员休息水平，遇需鏖战冲刺的阶段应做到以身作则以己为先，且对成员做恰当的奖励或足额的补偿。
  1. From the first planning pass, weigh how tasks and their intensity will hit people's mood and condition; balance the schedule against workload so the team gets a real mix of work and rest.
  2. Never bring negative feelings to work — the higher the position, the more emotional steadiness comes first; catch anxiety or restlessness in the crew early and stop it spreading.
  3. Review the work in stages as you go — but leave the Monday-morning quarterbacking out.
  4. Give instructions with a clear goal and purpose — never use people like machines by handing out tasks that are confusing or unexplained.
  5. Watch everyone's rest levels day to day; when a crunch calls for an all-out sprint, lead from the front and carry your own weight first, and give the crew proper recognition or full compensation.
